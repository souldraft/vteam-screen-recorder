Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Short version: the app has no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no account. Your recordings stay on your PC. Information is only sent to me when you choose to contact support or donate.

What stays on your PC

  • Your recordings are saved only to the folder you choose. The app doesn't upload them anywhere.
  • Camera and microphone are used only while the recording bar is open with the camera or mic switched on, and only to show you the picture and level and to record. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Settings (save folder, recorder options) are kept in a recorder-settings.json file next to the app.
  • Support ID: a random code like 7F3K-92QD, created on your PC the first time the app runs. It isn't based on your name, hardware or anything about you. It only helps me find your message when you contact me.

What the app connects to

  • FFmpeg install: winget (Microsoft) or FFmpeg's official GitHub release, only when you install it. Otherwise the app doesn't go online, except for the Support form when you send it.

When you contact support

If you send the 💬 Support form, the app sends:

  • the subject, your email address and your message;
  • your support ID, the app version, your Windows version and your FFmpeg version;
  • if you choose to include them: the app's recent log and a screenshot.

The form is passed through a small Google Apps Script that emails it to my Gmail inbox (vteam732@gmail.com). The script keeps only a short-lived counter (cleared within an hour) to stop spam floods. If the form can't send, the app opens your own email app instead. Google processes these emails under Google's privacy policy.

I use support messages only to answer you and fix problems. I don't sell or share them. Ask me and I'll delete your messages.

When you donate

  • Donations are paid in your own UPI app and go straight to my bank account. No payment company is involved, and the app and website never see your bank or UPI PIN details.
  • The tick box you confirm before donating isn't saved anywhere, in the app or on this site.
  • My bank shows me what it shows for any UPI payment: your name as registered with your bank, your UPI ID, the amount, the date and the note. I keep this only to recognise donations and handle refund requests for mistakes. I don't share or sell it.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security; see the GitHub privacy statement. The donation QR codes are plain pictures stored on this site.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Questions or deletion requests: vteam732@gmail.com.
