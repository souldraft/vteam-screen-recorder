Refund policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

The app is free, so there's nothing to refund for using it. Donations are voluntary gifts and are normally not refunded.

Mistakes I will refund

  • You paid twice by accident, or paid the wrong amount (for example ₹300 instead of ₹30).
  • Email me within 7 days at vteam732@gmail.com with the date, the amount and the UPI transaction ID (UTR or reference number, shown in your UPI app's payment history).
  • I'll send the mistaken amount back to the same UPI account, usually within 7 days of your email.

What isn't refunded

  • A donation you meant to make but later changed your mind about.
  • Donations older than 7 days, unless the law where you live says otherwise.

Donations go directly to my bank account through UPI; no payment company is involved, so I'm the one to contact. This policy doesn't affect any rights you have under the consumer laws of the country where you live.

vteam732@gmail.com, or 💬 Support in the app.
